ShopFlow AI

From messy orders to smarter purchasing decisions.

ShopFlow helps independent electrical retailers understand customer orders, verify inventory, detect supplier price changes and make purchasing decisions when cash is limited.

Not a chatbot. ShopFlow uses AI for understanding messy orders and asking for clarification. Deterministic code controls prices, inventory, budgets and purchasing decisions — the model never writes a business number.

Supplier prices — read a dealer price list

Photograph a supplier's price list. ShopFlow reads it, matches each line to a catalogue SKU, and compares the printed rate against what this shop last paid. A move of more than 5% is flagged. Reading a supplier price list does not automatically change the shop's cost. Extract → compare → you confirm → the confirmed cost is stored → the planner uses it. Nothing moves until you say so.

The workflow

  1. 1Order A contractor sends a spoken or typed order in everyday shop language. ShopFlow maps it to real catalogue SKUs — and asks when a request is ambiguous instead of guessing.
  2. 2Verify Each line is checked against the shop's current inventory state to find exactly what is short.
  3. 3Quote A clean quotation the owner can read out or share straight away.
  4. 4Supplier intelligence A photographed dealer price list is read and compared against what the shop paid before, surfacing increases that would otherwise pass unnoticed.
  5. 5Protect A confirmed increase is immediately measured against the selling price, so the owner sees what is left of the margin. ShopFlow never changes the selling price by itself.
  6. 6Budget The owner states the cash actually available. That constraint is real, and it binds.
  7. 7Decision A purchase plan: what to buy now, what to defer, and what deferring risks. Customer commitments come first.
  8. 8Why Every recommendation opens up into the full calculation behind it — stock, velocity, lead time, margin, budget remaining.

All eight steps are live above. Every figure comes from the deterministic engine — the language model never writes a number.

The trust principle

The AI never invents business numbers.

The language model understands the order, chooses which tool to call and asks for clarification. Every quantity, price, shortage and total is produced by a separate deterministic engine that cannot reach a model. SKU ids are rejected unless they exist in the catalogue, and any figure the model writes is checked against the engine's output before it is shown. That separation is enforced by the architecture and covered by tests, not asserted in a prompt.

The same line holds for anything consequential. ShopFlow does not change your selling price, does not place an order and does not message anyone. A supplier cost is recorded only when you confirm it, a margin warning is a recommendation you act on yourself, and the WhatsApp buttons open a draft for you to read and send.
